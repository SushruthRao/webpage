Welcome to Poorna Tattva

In the quiet rhythms of the earth and the laughter of little feet, there lies a space where learning is not rushed, but realized. At Poorna Tattva Play Home, education is life itself—unfolding gracefully like the petals of a lotus. Childhood here is not a phase to hurry, but a time to build a foundation deeply, joyfully, and meaningfully.

Vision

To awaken the full potential of every child by nurturing their natural curiosity, cultural roots, and inner harmony—creating mindful individuals who live with purpose, compassion, and a deep connection to the world around them.

Mission

  • 🌳 To offer holistic, experiential learning rooted in nature, tradition, and creativity
  • 🌳 To integrate ancient wisdom with contemporary relevance through play, art, language, and community life
  • 🌳 To cultivate self-awareness, ecological sensitivity, and a lifelong love for learning in every child

Philosophy

Our Roots: Indian Philosophy. Our Wings: Innovation.

At Poorna Tattva, we believe that the early years are sacred—where a child’s worldview begins to form. Guided by the timeless wisdom of Indian philosophy, we honor each child as a unique spark of potential, unfolding at their own rhythm. Inspired by principles such as Ahimsa (non-violence), Samskara (values), and Sahaj (naturalness), our learning environment is nurturing, mindful, and deeply respectful of the child’s innate nature. From these strong roots, we give our children wings to explore the world through creative play, storytelling, nature walks, music, and hands-on discovery. We blend ancient practices with modern pedagogical insights, creating a rich, stimulating space that fosters curiosity, confidence, and compassion. Here, education is not rushed or forced—it is lived, experienced, and celebrated.

🚌 Beyond Classroom Walls 🚌

Field trips

🏛️ Heritage sites 🏛️

Children explore ancient monuments, forts, and temples to connect with history, architecture, and storytelling. These visits deepen their sense of identity and cultural continuity. A visit to the zoo sparks wonder and curiosity as children observe animals up close in their naturalistic habitats. It nurtures empathy for living beings, strengthens ecological understanding, and brings science to life through real-world exploration.

🌲🌳🍃 Forest Trails 🌲🌳🍃

Guided walks through forests and natural landscapes awaken curiosity, observation, and reverence for the earth. They help children understand ecosystems while grounding them in nature's rhythm. Neighbourhood exploration - Children walk through nearby markets, places of worship, and hospitals to understand how communities function. These visits build social awareness, empathy, and a sense of belonging by connecting learning to real-life environments.

🎭 Theatre Workshops 🎭

Through roleplay, expression, and improvisation, children learn to articulate emotions, build confidence, and embody stories. Theatre fosters empathy, collaboration, and joyful communication.

🎵 Music Workshops 🎵

Children immerse in the magic of melody, rhythm, and sound through playful singing, traditional instruments, and joyful listening. Music becomes a gateway to emotional harmony, creativity, and deep inner balance.

💃 Dance Workshops 💃

Through graceful steps and joyful movement, children discover the power of expression and rhythm. Exploring classical and folk dances, they develop body awareness, confidence, and a deep connection to cultural roots.
